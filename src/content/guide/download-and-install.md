# Download & install

omanote runs on the web (phone included), on desktop, and as a browser extension. They all sync through the same encrypted account.

## Web app (recommended for most people)

The quickest way to start, with no installation needed. Open [omanote.com](https://omanote.com) in your browser and sign up. It works on any device with a modern browser.

**Install it as a PWA** to get a standalone window: in Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar, or use the menu → Install omanote. This adds omanote to your taskbar or home screen with offline support and faster loading, without an app store. On your phone, your browser's "Add to Home Screen" option does the same thing.

## Desktop app (macOS, Windows, Linux)

The desktop app runs omanote in a native window with its own tray icon, auto-updates, and OS integration. Download the latest installer from the [releases page](https://github.com/thehumanx/omanote-releases/releases/latest):

- **macOS:** a `.dmg` file. Open it and drag omanote to your Applications folder.
- **Windows:** an `.msi` installer. Run it and follow the prompts.
- **Linux:** `.deb` on Debian and Ubuntu, `.rpm` on Fedora, or `.AppImage` on Arch and anywhere else. The AppImage is a single file you make executable and run, with no installation. If it fails to start on a system without FUSE, run it as `APPIMAGE_EXTRACT_AND_RUN=1 ./omanote.AppImage`.

The desktop app checks for updates in the background every 30 minutes. When a new version is available, a banner appears at the bottom of the screen. Click it to download and restart.

## Browser extension (Chrome & Firefox)

The extension adds quick capture to any browser tab without switching windows. Install it from:

- [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/omanote/foafmfgfdbdiiggmmfdoalgpfhkejbjn) (also works in Edge, Brave, Opera, and other Chromium-based browsers)
- [Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/omanote/)

Once installed, click the toolbar icon or press **Alt+Shift+O** to open quick capture from any page. You can also right-click selected text to save it as a note or bookmark. The extension uses the same encryption as the main app, so nothing leaves your device unencrypted.

No account is needed to install the extension. Sign in once to sync with your canvas.

## On your phone

There's no native mobile app yet. The web app works fully on your phone: added to your home screen, it runs full-screen and offline, with push notifications on supported browsers.
