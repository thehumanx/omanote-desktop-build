# Profile & account

Your profile (name, email, and avatar) is managed through your account provider. omanote doesn't store a separate profile and uses what your account provides.

## Where to find your profile

Tap your avatar at the top of any page to open the profile menu. It stays in the same spot everywhere, including while scrolling or browsing Canvas history. Here you'll see:

- Your **display name** and **email address**
- An **Edit** link that opens your account settings
- **Explore**, Settings, and the rest of the app's menu items

## Changing your name or avatar

Click **Edit** in the profile menu. This opens `accounts.omanote.com/user` in a new tab, the Clerk-managed account page where you can update your display name, profile photo, and other account details. Changes you make there sync back to omanote automatically.

## What the greeting uses

Your display name feeds into the greeting on the canvas (see **Dynamic greetings**). If you haven't set a name, the greeting falls back to "there".

## Email in your profile

Your email is shown masked in the profile menu, for example `a***@example.com`, and is never displayed in full anywhere in the app. To change it, use the same Edit link to your account settings.
