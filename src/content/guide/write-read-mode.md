# Write & Read mode

When you enable the **RSS reader** in Settings → Features, omanote gains a second mode switch at the top of the app. **Write mode** is your daily canvas and **Read mode** is your feed reader. They share the same space, hashtags, and bookmarks.

## Write mode

The default mode. Here you see the canvas and the focused views (Todos, Notes, Bookmarks, Events). Press **+** or **/** at any time to open the composer and capture something.

## Read mode

When you switch to Read mode, the canvas tab bar changes to show **Feeds** and **Saved**. Feeds shows your RSS subscriptions, organized by category. Saved holds articles you've bookmarked from the reader. The layout is a three-pane desktop view: feed categories on the left, article list in the middle, and the full article on the right.

## Switching between them

On desktop, the mode switch is a small floating rail pinned to the left edge of the window. Hover it to see the "Write" and "Read" labels. On mobile it's a compact icon-only pill in the top bar next to the date. Tap either one to toggle. Whichever tab you were on in Write mode is preserved when you switch back.

The reader is off by default. Turn it on in Settings → Features when you want feeds in the app.
