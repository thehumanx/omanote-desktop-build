# Canvas pages

The daily canvas covers a single day. For something that grows over days or weeks, such as a trip itinerary, a project brief, or reading notes, use a **canvas page**: a titled document, separate from the daily canvas, that you can create as many of as you like.

## Creating one

Open the composer and choose **Create new page**. It opens immediately with an empty title and body. Give it a name, pick an icon if you want one, and start writing.

If you close an empty page without typing anything, it's discarded rather than left behind as a stray "Untitled page". Creating another one while an empty page already exists reopens that same page instead of creating duplicates.

## What goes in a page

Alongside regular text, headings, and lists, a page can hold:

- **Checklist items.** Type `/todo` or start a line with the checklist block. These are real todos: check one off here or from the Todos screen and it updates in both places. They land in the "Others" folder in Todos and stay out of your daily canvas feed, since the page already shows them.
- **Links.** Paste a URL on its own line and it becomes a saved-link card with a preview, the same as a bookmark saved anywhere else in omanote. It's filed under your "Saved" bookmarks.
- **Images.** Paste, drag in, or use `/image`. Large images are compressed automatically to keep your storage usage down (see **Storage**, in Settings). Hover an image to delete it or drag its corner to resize. The small text field below it is a caption.

Deleting a checklist item, link, or image from inside the page also removes the underlying todo, bookmark, or image, and deleting it elsewhere removes it from the page.

## Sharing a page

See **Public links** for how to publish a page as a read-only link anyone can open, and what that means for your privacy.

## Where pages show up

A page appears as a card in "Your today" on the day you created it, and afterwards under "Continue writing". Editing an old page never moves it to a different day.
