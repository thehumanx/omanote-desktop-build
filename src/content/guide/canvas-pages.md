# Canvas pages

The daily canvas is for the stream of a single day. Sometimes you want more room — a trip itinerary, a project brief, reading notes — something that grows over days or weeks and deserves its own space. That's a **canvas page**: a titled document, separate from the daily canvas, that you can create as many of as you like.

## Creating one

Open the composer and choose **Create new page**. It opens immediately with an empty title and body — give it a name, pick an icon if you like, and start writing.

If you close an empty page without typing anything in it, it's discarded rather than left behind as a stray "Untitled page." Creating another one while an empty page already exists reopens that same page instead of piling up duplicates.

## What goes in a page

Alongside regular text, headings, and lists, a page can hold:

- **Checklist items** — type `/todo` or start a line with the checklist block. These are real todos: check one off here or from the Todos screen and it updates in both places. They land in the "Others" folder in Todos, and don't also show up in your daily canvas feed — the page is where they live.
- **Links** — paste a URL on its own line and it becomes a saved-link card with a preview, the same as a bookmark saved anywhere else in omanote. It's filed under your "Saved" bookmarks.
- **Images** — paste, drag in, or use `/image`. Large images are automatically shrunk to keep your storage usage down (see **Storage**, in Settings). Hover an image to delete it or drag its corner to resize; the small text field below it is a caption.

Deleting a checklist item, link, or image from inside the page removes the underlying todo, bookmark, or image too — and vice versa.

## Where pages show up

A page appears as a card in "Your today" on the day you created it, and later under "Continue writing" if you go back to it. Editing an old page never moves it to a different day.
