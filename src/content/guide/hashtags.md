# Hashtags & Explore

Hashtags connect items across types. Tag a note, a todo, and an event with the same `#tag` and omanote groups them together.

## Tagging

Type `#` in any note, todo, or event and an autocomplete list of your existing tags appears. Pick one or make a new one. Tags render as colored chips, and each tag keeps the same color wherever it shows up.

## The Explore map

The **Explore** view draws your hashtags as a mindmap-style graph, showing which tags connect to which items and to each other. Click a tag to jump to everything filed under it.

## Why it helps

Folders organize by where something lives; hashtags organize by what it's about. A `#project-x` tag gathers the meeting notes, the follow-up todos, and the events from that project, even when they're different artifact types in different folders.
