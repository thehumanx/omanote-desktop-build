# Capturing anything

The composer is the single place you put things in. Press **+** in the navigation bar, or **/** anywhere in the app, to open it from wherever you are. It reads what you type and turns it into the matching kind of item, so you rarely have to stop and choose.

Closing it, either with **Esc** or by clicking elsewhere, never saves anything. Whatever you typed is still there the next time you open the composer, so it's safe to close and come back later. If you had something in progress, reopening the composer keeps showing that draft even when it doesn't match the page you're on. Everything saves the same way: **Enter**. Notes used to be the exception, needing Cmd/Ctrl + Enter — they don't any more. **Shift + Enter** is the newline everywhere, whether that's a line break in a note or the next row in a list of todos.

Otherwise, a fresh composer matches the page you're on. Press **/** on Todos and it opens ready to make a todo, on Bookmarks a bookmark, and so on. If you have a Todos, Notes, or Bookmarks folder open, it defaults into that folder too.

## It figures out what you mean

- **Just type** a thought and save. It becomes a **note**.
- **Paste a link.** It becomes a **bookmark**, with a preview fetched automatically.
- **Start with `@`** for an **event** (something that happened). This works on the mobile app; on web and desktop, use `/event` instead.

## Slash commands

Type `/` to pick a type explicitly. The available commands are:

- `/todo` for a task, with optional due date and time
- `/event` for a logged event
- `/bookmark` for a saved link

Pick one and keep typing; your text carries over.

## Natural language

When you're making a todo or event, you can write the timing in plain language and omanote parses it:

- `Call the dentist tomorrow 4pm`
- `Submit report friday`
- `Team lunch in 2 hours`

Recurring phrases work too, such as `every day`, `every mon and fri`, `every month on the 5th`, or `every month on the last saturday`. See **Recurring todos & reminders** for the full set.

## Hashtags and emoji

Type `#` anywhere to tag an item. An autocomplete list of your existing tags appears, and tags show as colored chips. Type `:` to insert an emoji by name. Both work in notes, todos, and events.

## Capturing several at once

For todos and events, each line becomes its own item, so you can enter a whole checklist at once and save it together.
