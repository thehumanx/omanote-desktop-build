# Getting started

omanote is built around a single idea: **your day is the unit**. Todos, notes, bookmarks, events, and articles you save all land on one daily **canvas**, so everything you capture stays grouped by when you captured it.

## Capture first, organize later

Press **+** in the navigation bar, or **/** anywhere in the app, to open the composer. It reads what you type:

- Type a thought and press save. It becomes a **note**.
- Paste a link. It becomes a **bookmark** with a preview.
- Prefix with `@` for an **event** (something that happened). Mobile app only for now; on web and desktop, use `/event`.
- Type `/` to pick todo, event, or bookmark explicitly.

You can sort things into folders and add hashtags later, so nothing has to be filed at the moment you write it down.

## Moving between days

The canvas always shows one day. Use the date strip at the top to move backward and forward. Yesterday's canvas still holds yesterday's todos, notes, and events exactly as you left them, so past days stay readable as a record.

## Your passphrase keeps everything private

Everything you write is **encrypted on your device** before it's saved to the cloud, so only you can read it. That also makes your passphrase important. During setup, omanote gives you a **recovery key**. Save it somewhere safe: if you forget your passphrase and don't have the recovery key, your encrypted content cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.

## Use it everywhere

omanote runs as a web app, a native app for Android and iOS, a desktop app, and a browser extension for saving links and snippets from any page. Everything syncs through the same account and works offline: changes queue locally and sync when you reconnect.

The **Download & install** page covers every platform. The **Todos** section covers due dates, reminders, and recurring tasks. **Notes**, **Bookmarks**, **Events**, and **Canvas pages** each have their own guide.
