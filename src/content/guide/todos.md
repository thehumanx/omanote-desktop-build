# Todos

Todos are your task ledger. The **Todos** view is where you manage all of them across time, rather than one day at a time on the canvas.

## Creating a todo

Make one from the composer (`/todo`, or start a checklist line) or from the Todos view. Add a due date and time in plain language, such as `pay bills friday 9am`, and omanote sets the schedule for you.

## Editing a todo

Hover a todo and click the pencil icon, or double-click it, to open the full editor: title, due date, folder, repeat, and reminders in one place. This works the same way everywhere a todo shows up, including the Todos view, the daily canvas, and the Event/Calendar screen. On mobile it opens as a floating drawer sized for touch. Long-press a todo on the canvas to open it, then tap the checkmark or the X to save or cancel.

## Due dates, reminders, and priority

- **Due date & time:** todos with no date default to today. Add a time to get a reminder.
- **Reminders:** when a todo's time arrives, omanote shows an in-app toast and, if you allow it, a browser or device notification. You can snooze or complete from the reminder itself.
- **Priority:** mark a todo high-priority to make it stand out.

## Active and Done

Each folder has two tabs: **Active** and **Done**. Active groups every open todo by day: overdue first (each group labeled "Overdue by N days"), then today, then everything scheduled later. What needs attention soonest stays at the top, so you don't have to switch tabs to check for overdue work. Done shows everything you've completed, most recent first.

## Folders

Organize todos into folders with custom icons (a Lucide icon or an emoji). Uncategorized todos live in a default **Others** folder. Each folder shows a completed/total count badge, which turns into a green checkmark once everything inside is done. You can sort folders alphabetically, by last updated, or by number of todos, and share a folder as a public link.

On mobile, opening a folder slides in a full page from the right instead of a bottom sheet. Swipe from the left edge or tap the back arrow to return to the folder list. The folder's Share button sits next to the "•••" menu, which holds Rename and Delete. A floating "+" at the bottom adds a new todo into that folder.

## Completing a todo

Checking a todo off marks it done and logs it into your **Events** timeline as a past-tense record ("Paid bills"), so completed work shows up alongside everything else from that day.

## Recurring todos

Todos can repeat on a schedule: daily, weekly, monthly, or patterns like "the last Saturday of every month". That has its own guide, **Recurring todos & reminders**.
