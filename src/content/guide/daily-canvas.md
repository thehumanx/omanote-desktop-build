# The daily canvas

The canvas is omanote's home. It shows **one day at a time**: every todo, note, bookmark, and event tied to that day, together in one scrollable feed. It's where you capture things and where you review them later.

## One day, everything together

A quick note, the link you saved, the task you finished, and the thing that happened all sit side by side on one day, in the order you captured them.

## Moving between days

The date strip at the top moves you backward and forward through your days. Today is where you capture, past days keep what you already recorded, and future days show scheduled todos and reminders.

## Arranging your day

Items land on the canvas in the order you captured them, oldest to newest, so new entries sit at the bottom and nothing needs rearranging. You can toggle the dot grid on or off in Settings → Appearance.

## What lands here

Anything you capture from the composer shows up on the canvas for the current day. Some things also appear on other days: a todo shows on its due date, and a recurring todo appears on every day it's due. A todo due later also shows on the day you wrote it, labelled with its date (*Todo for Fri, Oct 2*), and a recurring todo that starts later shows the same way (*Starts Wed, Sep 30 · every month on the 30th*).

Completing a todo logs it into your timeline, which shows up as an event on the day you ticked it. The todo itself stays on its own day, marked done with the date you finished it, so finishing last week's task today doesn't move it: last week shows it done, and today shows that you did it.

Each item appears on the day you created it. Editing a note or a page later doesn't bring it back onto today's canvas.

## Overdue

Open todos whose date has passed collect in the **Overdues** section at the top of today's canvas, so they don't stay buried on the day you missed them. Tick one off there, or move it to today or next week.

A recurring todo shows there too, once: if you missed its latest occurrence, it waits in Overdues until the next one is due, then that one takes over in today's list. A missed daily never lands there, because tomorrow's is always next. Recurring todos can't be moved to another day from here, so tick them off or open the series.
