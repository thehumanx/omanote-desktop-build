# RSS reader

omanote includes an optional RSS reader, so the writing you follow sits in the same app as everything else. It's off by default. Turn it on in **Settings → Features**.

## Subscribing to feeds

Add a feed by its URL, or the address of a site that publishes one. Organize your subscriptions into categories, the same folder idea used across omanote.

## Reading

Feeds show their latest items in a familiar list-and-reader layout: pick a feed or category on the left, read on the right. Items you've read are tracked, and you can mark a whole feed as read to clear the backlog.

## Saving articles

Save an article to come back to it later. Saved articles are kept in their own view, separate from the unread stream.

## Images and privacy

Pictures in articles come from the sites that publish them, so loading one tells that site you're reading. omanote keeps that to a minimum:

- **Tracking pixels are always removed.** Newsletters hide tiny invisible images to learn when you opened an issue. They never load in omanote.
- **No "came from omanote".** Images are requested without saying which site asked for them, and only once you scroll to them.
- **Block images in articles.** Turn this on in **Settings → Features** to open articles with no pictures at all. A note at the top says how many were held back, and **Load images** shows them for that article.
